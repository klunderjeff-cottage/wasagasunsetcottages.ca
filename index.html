Welcome

Nestled among towering white pines, our property is one of the closest cottage locations to Beach 1 — no parking fees, no long walks, just pure convenience.
If you’re already on the sand and realize you forgot your towel or sunscreen, being this close means you can simply pop back to the cottage and return within minutes.

Why book a single room when you can enjoy an entire cottage?
Wasaga Beach has everything you need for a great stay: restaurants, coffee shops, grocery stores, and plenty of activities. Spend the day mini-putting, go-karting, renting jet skis, touring on a licensed pontoon tiki bar, or exploring miles of scenic hiking trails.

If you’ve never visited Wasaga before, Beach 1 is the heart of the action — the busiest and most vibrant stretch, full of shops, restaurants, and people. Our property is just a 2‑minute walk away. You’ll see families strolling by with towels, beach bags, umbrellas, and everything that makes summer at Wasaga unforgettable.
