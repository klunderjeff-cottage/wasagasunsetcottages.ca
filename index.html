Welcome

The space Nestled among towering white pine trees, we are one of the closest cottages to Beach 1. No need to pay for parking as you can walk to the beach from the cottage. Even if you are at the beach and forgot your towel or sunscreen, being this close to the beach is perfect because you can just run back and get it. Why pay for a room when you can get an entire cottage! The town of Wasaga Beach has plenty to offer including restaurants, coffee shops, grocery stores etc. For fun you can mini putt, go kart, rent jet ski's tour on a licensed pontoon tiki bar, or explore miles of hiking trails! If you’ve never been to Wasaga before, beach 1 is the busiest of them all. It is the prime spot for shops, restaurants and people. Our property is a 2 minute walk to Beach 1. You will see families walking by all day long carrying their towels, beach bags, umbrellas etc.
